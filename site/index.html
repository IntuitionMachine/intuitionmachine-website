The core idea

One operator, and everything else is built from it

The theory models cognition as movement on a small structured space. There is a ground state — unformed potential — and from it three levels unfold in a fixed order: raw felt quality, then brute encounter, then mediated convention.

Everything at those levels comes in four attentional flavours: inner-personal, outer-physical, social, and formal-systemic. Two operations climb and descend the levels; two symmetries swap the flavours. That is the entire machinery.

Every construct in the theory — the recurring loops organisations run, the ways they go wrong, how tradeoffs resolve — is built from those pieces. A program (verify_qml.py) checks the formal claims exhaustively in a small finite model.

The point of the construction is honesty about foundations. Rather than resting on a long list of philosophical commitments, the theory rests on explicit mathematical assumptions whose consequences a program can verify.

The central result

The worst failure is a one-letter edit that still compiles

An audit asks whether a claim matches the observation. In kernel terms: fold the claim down to the encounter level, move it into the observation's column, and take the two-sided difference. Delete a single letter and it checks the story against another story.

This is why narrative capture is cheap. In this theory a pathology is not damage — it is a minimal silent edit that preserves apparent function. Which also means the route back is short: because edits at the spine are impossible, every quality-bypass is a routing failure around an intact capacity.

An audit office that checks reports against other reports. The fold is gone, and nobody noticed because the reports kept arriving.

Provenance substitution

Coordinating on last year

Output credulity

Route substitution  ·  new in 5.3

The misroute

Every process performs correctly and the content reached the wrong one. Each loop passes all seven diagnostic layers individually; the outcome is still wrong. A complaint handled competently as operations, never reaching intelligence.

Binding decay  ·  new in 5.3

Orphaned convention

The form still completed in triplicate because a system decommissioned in 2011 required it. Its paper trail is impeccable — in 2009 the rule was fully justified. What is missing is not history but a present-tense owner empowered to notice the world has drifted.

Method

Every claim says what kind of support it has

Four labels run through the specification, so a reader always knows which register a statement is in — and which parts can be detached without taking the rest down.

Axiom Theorem Semi-formal Interpretation

Axioms are postulated, with intellectual debts named. Theorems are proved or exhaustively verified in the canonical model. Semi-formal statements need a model family not yet constructed. Interpretation is the organisational reading — severable from the formal claims.

Open problems are numbered. Flagged anomalies are flagged rather than silently fixed. One 5.3 addition ships with its own kill-condition: if healthy editorless conventions turn out to be common, the condition is falsified and demotes to a heuristic.

New in 5.3

Dispatch — the moment content is routed to a handler

A large share of real organisational failure lives in an unmodelled moment: the theory could say exactly what each loop would do with an arriving item, but not how the item is assigned to one.

Sel

The selector

The system's front desk: the standing function that takes arriving content and engages a loop. It is typed, it has a provenance regime — compiled rules or live judgment — and where compiled, its rule set is itself a convention that needs a witness and an owner.

V16

Editor liveness

Freezing a judgment into a rule does not destroy the judging; it relocates it to whoever can edit the rule. When that person retires, the judgment vanishes while the rule keeps executing. Every standing convention needs a living owner, not just an evidence trail.

⌈ ⌉

The ceiling conjecture

How far a process can ever be standardised is capped by how deep into felt experience its work descends. Management never goes below encounter — and proceduralises well. Grounding reaches quality, so it can promise the same meaning, never the same steps.
