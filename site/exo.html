ExO 3.0

From ExO 3.0 to production

ExO 3.0 shows organisations what the AI-native future looks like. Intuition Machine helps them build it.

The execution gap

A clear destination, and decades of systems in the way

ExO 3.0 describes organisations that run through coordinated networks of people, AI agents, shared intelligence and continuous learning. It sets out an Intelligence Stack, a GOVERN/ASSURE control plane from the outset, and the REWRITE method for getting there — starting with a small AI-native Edge Twin that runs real work alongside existing operations.

Existing enterprises have to make that journey from where they are: decades of technology investment, regulation, policy and organisational dependency. Translating the architecture into working systems under those conditions is engineering work. That is the work we do.
