Interactive atlas

Semiosis Atlas

Sign-making, mapped. Two synchronised views — a classification field of 66 sign classes and a temporal process spiral — trace how attention, competing interpretants, contextual constraint and provisional understanding build into meaning and action. Play a scenario, or read it as the transformer analogy for how a language model settles on sense.

4D Chess

Chess lifted into four dimensions: pieces move across four linked 4×4×2×2 boards under movement rules extended along the extra axes. Guided lessons, checkmate studies and game replay come with it, against the computer or a second player — a hands-on way to feel how structure multiplies once you add dimensions.
